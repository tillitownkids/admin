# UI/UX critique: TilliTown admin panel (whole app)

Making one episode is a single job, but the app spreads it across nine sidebar pages plus a second five-step wizard, and asks the user to re-pick the episode on almost every one.

## Design health score

| # | Heuristic | Score | Key issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Visibility of system status | 2 | No single view of where each episode stands; breadcrumb says "Page" on detail screens |
| 2 | Match with the real world | 2 | System wording: "Saved to DB!", "Fetching stories from database", "Magnific identifier" |
| 3 | User control and freedom | 2 | Regenerating overwrites saved work with no undo |
| 4 | Consistency and standards | 1 | 93 hand-built buttons vs 13 shared ones; 5 corner radii; 4 page widths |
| 5 | Error prevention | 2 | "Generate all" spends credits in one click, no count-and-cost confirmation |

| 9 | Error recovery | 2 | 4 "Operation successful" messages and top-of-page banners remain |
| 10 | Help and documentation | 1 | Nothing says what order to work in |

## Design specificity
Generic dark admin template (neutral greys, one green, card grids). The product's real shape, an episode moving through a pipeline, appears only inside one page's stepper. Detector: gradient-text in src/components/PageHeader.tsx (every page title); browser overlay on /login: dark-glow coloured box-shadow.

## What's working
- Pipeline order is right; the production stepper locks later steps.
- New characters and locations are reviewed before saving; brainstorm chat needs an explicit "Merge Changes".
- Loading states on every screen; one restrained colour theme.

## Priority issues
- [P1] One job, fourteen places. Sidebar: Story Generate, Characters, Locations, Script Generate, Storyboard, Video Production, Video Stitching, Video Publishing; Video Production adds 5 steps, three of which (Locations, References, Scenes) only re-confirm earlier work. Fix: sidebar of Episodes, Library, Settings; one episode page with one stepper Story > Script > Storyboard > Video > Publish; remove confirm-only steps. Command: /impeccable shape
- [P1] Recall instead of recognition. Script, Storyboard, Video Production and Stitching each start by re-picking the story or script; cards show a 50-character concept and all say "Episode 1". Fix: Episodes list with status column and Continue button; real titles and numbers. Command: /impeccable shape
- [P1] Screens show everything at once. Dashboard: 4 stat cards, credits, global settings form, activity, quick actions. Script screen: cast grid, location grid and full story above one button. Story form: 13 buttons. Fix: one primary action per screen; settings on own page; credits as header chip; collapse reference material. Command: /impeccable distill
- [P2] Flat hierarchy and inconsistent components. 80% of text is text-xs/text-sm, font-bold 95 times, uppercase labels, emerald hard-coded 101 times. Fix: three text levels, bold for titles only, shared Button/Card/Badge everywhere. Command: /impeccable layout, /impeccable typeset
- [P2] Expensive and destructive actions look ordinary. Generate All Storyboards, Generate remaining clips, regenerating storyboards over saved scenes. Fix: confirm with count and estimated credits; plain wording. Command: /impeccable harden, /impeccable clarify

## Persona red flags
- Alex (power user): nine page hops, 4-5 re-selections per episode; no search in Characters/Locations; no end-to-end run.
- Sam (keyboard/screen reader): clickable div cards not keyboard reachable; 6 aria-labels in the app; 10-11px text; status by colour alone.
- Jordan (new teammate): sidebar order implies Characters after Story; "Video Production" opens "Episode Production" starting at Locations.

## Minor observations
- 260px sidebar cannot collapse; no mobile navigation.
- 0.4s page fade on every navigation; hover scale on cards.
- Native confirm() for deletes.
- /location-new is not linked from anywhere.
- Page titles mix Generator, Generation, Brainstorm, Production.

## Questions to consider
- What if the app had only three places: Episodes, Library, Settings?
- Does anyone act on the four dashboard numbers?
- Should the steps nobody changes be automatic?
